ABOUT ACCA

A Globally Respected Qualification for Finance Professionals.

ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) is the global body for professional accountants, offering a qualification that combines technical expertise with strategic business acumen.

ACCA EXAMINATIONS (STRUCTURE)

The Clear Roadmap to Professional Qualification.

01 FIRST LEVEL

Applied Knowledge

3 Papers (Builds the Foundation)
  • BT - Business & Technology
  • MA - Management Accounting
  • FA - Financial Accounting
Objective (MCQs)
02 SECOND LEVEL

Applied Skills

6 Papers (Core Tech Expertise)
  • LW - Corporate & Business Law (GLO)
  • TX - Taxation (UK)
  • PM - Performance Management
  • PR - Financial Reporting
  • AA - Audit & Assurance
  • FM - Financial Management
Descriptive + Objective (MCQs)
03 FINAL LEVEL

Strategic Professional

4 Papers (Leadership & Strategy)
  • Essentials (2 Papers)
  • SBL - Strategic Business Leader
  • SBR - Strategic Business Reporting
  • Options (Choose 2 out of 4)
  • AFM - Advanced Financial Management
  • APM - Advanced Performance Management
  • ATX - Advanced Taxation (UK)
  • AAA - Advanced Audit & Assurance (INT)
Descriptive
Entry Requirement: 12th Pass (Commerce) - 50% Marks
Exam Mode: Computer Based (CBE)
Exam Centres: ACCA Approved Centers
